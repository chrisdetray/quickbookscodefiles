# Privacy Policy

**Application:** ATD Bill Automation
**Effective Date:** July 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the ATD Bill Automation application ("the App") handles data. The App is a private, internal tool and is not made available to the public.

## 1. Data Collected

The App accesses:
- Email attachments (vendor bills/receipts) from a designated Gmail inbox, via the Gmail API.
- QuickBooks Online accounting data (vendors, bills), via the Intuit Developer API.

## 2. How Data Is Used

Data collected is used exclusively to automate the entry of vendor bills into QuickBooks Online for ATD's internal accounting purposes. No data collected by the App is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

## 3. Data Storage

Authentication tokens (OAuth credentials for Gmail and QuickBooks Online) are stored locally and securely, and are not transmitted to any third party beyond Google and Intuit as required for API authentication.

## 4. Data Retention

Bill and vendor data processed by the App remains within QuickBooks Online and Gmail, governed by their respective account settings. The App itself does not maintain a separate long-term data store beyond what is needed for processing.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with:
- Google Gmail API (governed by Google's Privacy Policy)
- Intuit QuickBooks Online API (governed by Intuit's Privacy Policy)
